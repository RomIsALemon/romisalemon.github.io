Tactical RPG Project

Tools Used:

  • Unreal
  • Blueprint Scripting
  • Aseprite

This is a grid-based Tactical RPG with HD2D Graphics, inspired by Fire Emblem. While this will take quite a long time, I've already learned a lot from working on this regarding camera movement, grid/tile systems and pathfinding. This is by far my most complicated project yet, but I'm looking forward to how everything will come together in the final product!

Point and Click Detective Project

Tools Used:

  • Unreal
  • Blueprint Scripting
  • C++
  • Aseprite

Our team project is a point and click detective game where you play as the murderer as you investigate the crime scene and attempt to cover your tracks. I ended up using some of what I learned from the tactical RPG project to make a working point and click camera system. I'm super excited for this one, as we will be able to focus a lot on setting a dark and tense atmosphere as you try and get away with it in time...
